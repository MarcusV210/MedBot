Q1: What causes hypertension?
A1: [3 base models + Llama 70B backup]

    factors affecting cardiac output and vascular resistance...

Q2: What are the treatment options for it?
    ↑ (AI understands "it" = hypertension)
A2: 🟢 Context-Aware (ChatPDF-style)
    Treatment includes lifestyle modifications (DASH diet, sodium
    restriction, exercise) and medications (ACE inhibitors, ARBs,

Q3: Tell me more about ACE inhibitors
    ↑ (Continues the hypertension treatment discussion)
A3: 🟢 Context-Aware (ChatPDF-style)
    ACE inhibitors work by blocking angiotensin-converting enzyme,
    reducing angiotensin II production. Common examples include
    lisinopril, enalapril, ramipril...

Q4: What are the side effects?
    ↑ (Knows we're talking about ACE inhibitors)
A4: 🟢 Context-Aware (ChatPDF-style)
    Common side effects include dry cough (10-15% of patients),
    hyperkalemia, hypotension, and rarely angioedema...
